# Scope

The Open Sports Activity Exchange (OSAX): specifications for exchanging sport activities between
clubs, booking systems, platforms, federations and aggregators. This covers:

- publishing and retrieving activities (free court times, matches, events, tournaments, leagues,
  lessons, courses) with their venues, courts, prices, levels and conditions;
- booking players into those activities, quoting their prices, cancelling bookings and reporting
  cancellations and resales;
- exchanging match results;
- identifying players across systems and checking, with the player's consent, their membership of
  a club or federation;
- the lists these specifications refer to: sports, rating systems with their mapping of levels to
  percentiles, and court properties. How a level is converted from one rating system to another is
  left to implementations.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
